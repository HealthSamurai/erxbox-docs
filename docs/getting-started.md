# Getting Started with eRxbox

eRxbox provides electronic prescribing and medication management capabilities for healthcare applications.

## What is eRxbox?

eRxbox provides:

- **E-Prescribing** - Create and send electronic prescriptions
- **Medication Management** - Track patient medications
- **Drug Database** - Access drug information and interactions
- **Pharmacy Network** - Connect to pharmacy networks

## Quick Start

1. Contact us at [health-samurai.io/contacts](https://health-samurai.io/contacts) to get access
2. Configure your pharmacy network connections
3. Integrate e-prescribing into your workflow
